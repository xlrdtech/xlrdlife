Product Overview

LifeNote

“Whatever you speak automatically comes to life on its own.”

A breath-first, auto-manifesting life management system.

The inversion

The premise is Death Note turned inside out — from death to creation. Where that notebook ended lives, this one starts them. LifeNote captures the living breath of whatever you speak and brings it to life as organized, actionable, memory-persistent notes.

Core philosophy

Breath-First

All input originates from spoken breath, not mechanical capture.

Auto-Manifest

Spoken content becomes structured notes and suggestions with no manual formatting.

Canonical Design

One design language across every interface. No visual fragmentation, anywhere.

Memory-Persistent

Everything is searchable and retrievable, with attribution and context intact.

Action-Triggered

Notes are not static. They fire workflows, automations and agent executions.

Breath-to-life pipeline

Capture

Living breath, via wearable, phone or ambient presence.

Transcription

Real-time speech to text with speaker identification and context tagging.

Understanding

Intent is parsed — note, reminder, task, code, creative, action.

Manifestation

Content is auto-formatted into canonical markdown structure.

Storage

Written to canonical storage and distributed across memory layers.

Action

Associated workflows fire — calendar, agents, delivery, alerts.

What it writes for you

Six kinds of note, none of which you author by hand.

Daily notes · compiled by time and topic Reminders · from temporal references Meeting suggestions · inferred from context Action items · extracted, auto-routed Creative captures · lyrics, ideas, concepts Dev notes · specs, architecture, APIs

Design law

Written into the spec, not decided per screen: a single design language across app, terminal and wearable display. Dark by default with a light toggle. Liquid-glass aesthetic. No demo quality — production-grade visuals only.
