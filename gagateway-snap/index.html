Which path applies

The whole decision turns on one date

When did the certification period end?

Georgia does not decide renew-vs-new by how you feel about the case. It decides by counting days from the last day of the certification period. That single date sits in the notice inside the Gateway account — and it is the first thing to go read.

Path A — Late renewal

Ended 30 days ago or less

It is still a renewal. The existing case is picked back up rather than started from scratch, and benefits are prorated from the day the form lands.

"A late renewal is an application filed within 30 days following the expired certification period if the AU fails to file a renewal application in the last month of the certification period. Forms received in the month following the last month of the current certification period are treated as late renewals, and benefits are prorated from the date the valid form is received." PAMMS 3710 Recertifications (Renewals) — effective June 2026
Path B — New application

Ended more than 30 days ago

PAMMS 3710 defines a late renewal as within 30 days only. Past that window the policy provides no renewal route, so the case is restarted as a fresh application for benefits through Gateway.

Flagged honestly: PAMMS states the 30-day renewal window explicitly, but does not spell out the "past 30 days = new application" sentence in one line. Confirm it on the call — it costs one question and it decides which button to press.

Either path can be expedited. This is the part most people miss. PAMMS 3710 states plainly: "AUs that reapply after an expired certification period or within the 30 days following the last month of the certification period may be eligible for expedited services." A lapsed case is not disqualified from the 7-day track — it is specifically named as eligible for it.

Do this now, in order

  1. Log in and read My Notices

    This is the only step that must come first, because it produces the date that picks the path. Look for the certification end date and any closure notice.

    LOGIN Manage My Account or Logingateway.ga.gov

    Gateway is available Monday–Friday, 5 a.m. to midnight, per DFCS. Same login also shows benefits, lets you report changes and upload documents.

  2. Call the DFCS Customer Contact Center

    Do this even if the notice was clear. One call settles renew-vs-new, confirms whether the case can be reinstated, and starts the expedited screening. Say the words: "my case closed and I want to know if I can still file a late renewal or if I need a new application — and please screen me for expedited service."

    CALL (877) 423-4746DFCS Customer Contact Center ALT 1-800-GEORGIA · (800) 436-7442general State of Georgia line

  3. File the right form the same day

    Filing early matters mechanically, not just morally: benefits are prorated from the date the valid form is received, so every day of delay is food subtracted from the first payment.

    FILE Apply for Benefitsgateway.ga.gov · use for Path B PDF Form 508 — SNAP / Medicaid / TANF Renewaldfcs.georgia.gov · use for Path A

    An application counts as filed once it carries a name, address, date and signature — it does not have to be complete to start the clock. File it incomplete rather than late.

  4. Complete the interview — you have to make this call

    An interview is required on both paths. Georgia now runs "On-Demand" interviews: no appointment is mailed, and nothing happens until you phone in. Waiting for a scheduled call that never comes is the single most common way a case dies.

    "Even though these customers will not receive scheduled appointments, an interview is still required. The customers must call the agency to complete the interview within the expedited SOP timeframe to receive expedited services." PAMMS 3110 Expedited Application Processing

    Missed one already? It gets rescheduled on request — but you have to ask. Telephone by default; a face-to-face interview is available if you ask for it.

  5. Upload the documents through Gateway

    Upload beats mail and beats dropping them off — it timestamps instantly and lands in the case file. Photograph each document in daylight, flat, all four corners visible.

  6. Find the county office only if you need hands-on help

The 7-day track — ask for it by name

Expedited service means benefits inside 7 calendar days.

Every SNAP application in Georgia must be screened for it, and applications filed through Gateway are screened automatically. But knowing the criteria means you can state up front that you meet them.

Trigger 1
Less than $150 in monthly gross countable income and liquid resources not over $100.
Trigger 2
Monthly shelter and utility costs exceed monthly gross countable income plus liquid resources. This one catches far more people than they expect.
Trigger 3
Destitute migrant or seasonal farm worker with liquid resources not over $100.
Speed
Must be able to participate by the 7th calendar day following the application date. EBT card, PIN and benefits all available by then.
Proof needed
Identity only, before benefits are issued. Everything else can be postponed. Verification of identity must be issued by the 6th day.
Repeatable
There is no limit on how many times a household can be certified expedited, provided previously postponed verification was completed.
The catch that voids it. If identity is not verified, the interview is not completed, or a collateral contact is not given within the expedited timeframe, the case drops to normal 30-day processing and loses the postponed-verification right. And postponed documents carry no grace period — miss the end of that first or second month and benefits stop there.

What only you can do

  • Log in. I did not sign in, create anything, or submit a single field. The account is untouched — preparation only.
  • Read the notice and get the certification end date. Everything on this page branches off that one number, and it is only visible from inside the account.
  • Make the interview call. On-Demand interviews never call you. This cannot be delegated or automated.
  • Enter the personal fields yourself — SSN, date of birth, address, household members, income figures. Straight into Gateway, never into a page or a message.
  • State the expedited criteria out loud if shelter and utilities exceed income. Do not wait to be asked.
  • Say if either login email is dead. If neither address gets you in, the contact center can recover access.
