What makes it read as glass
Ring, never a disc. The spectrum is masked to a 1.5%-wide annulus. Filled in, it reads as a rainbow button instead of light refracting at an edge.

Bloom is the same gradient, blurred. One conic gradient painted twice — sharp on top, blurred and scaled behind. A separate glow colour never matches and looks pasted on.

The glyph carries a gradient. Flat fill reads as a sticker; a vertical ramp reads as machined metal. Visible at real size, invisible in a thumbnail.

Colour comes only from light. Base stays monochrome, which is why this transplants onto the green UI without fighting the palette.

Rim light does the depth. One inset top highlight plus an inset bottom shadow. Without the highlight the capsule stays flat however dark the fill gets.
