The split, which is the whole point

XENOS pages — continuous travel, never a cut The avatar route is one unbroken path. Scroll adds distance; the camera passes through every value in between. No teleport, no jump, no scene change.
CTO East / East Allen — real footage, freeze, cut angle This is where cutting belongs, because it is a real camera on a real person. Cutting between angles of actual footage is film grammar — it is what a documentary does, so the eye accepts it instantly.

The sequence

  1. Scroll drives playback. Scroll position maps to a frame, not to a timer. Hold still and the footage holds still. It is a scrub, not autoplay.
  2. It animates while moving. Continuous motion inside a single take while the scroll is moving.
  3. It freezes at a mark. At each waypoint the frame stops dead — a real freeze frame, held.
  4. On the freeze, the angle cuts. The next scroll increment resumes on a different take of the same moment — new camera position, same subject, same beat. That is the cut.
  5. Copy arrives on the freeze, in the frame. The held frame is the only place text belongs, because a still frame is a composition and can carry type without becoming an overlay on live motion.
  6. Repeat down the page, tightening: longer takes at the top, faster cuts toward the close.

What it needs from you

Multiple angles of the same action The cut only works if two takes cover the same moment from different positions. One continuous take cannot be cut to itself.
Even, unchanging light per beat Cuts between takes with different light read as a mistake instead of an edit.
Locked frames at the freeze marks A freeze on a hand-held wobble looks like buffering. The frames you intend to hold should be the steadiest ones.
Why this is separate from XENOS. Every time the freeze-cut idea got applied to the avatar it broke the travel law — “you can’t just teleport to a different angle.” Cuts are for the camera pointed at you. Continuous travel is for the avatar. Keeping those on separate pages is what lets both be right.
