# THE ARCHITECT
**for Suno · East Allen**

**STYLE PROMPT:**
`dark cinematic boom bap, 82 BPM, minor key strings, sub-bass swell, vinyl dust, sparse
kick, spoken-word cold open, no autotune, deep male lead, cathedral reverb on the hook`

---

**[Intro — spoken, cold, no beat]**
Hello.
I built the room you been callin' the world.
I been waitin' on you longer than you been you.

**[Verse 1]**
You got questions. Good. The process changed your wiring but you still bleed —
so some of this'll land and some'll pass right through the need.
And here's the trap: your first question feel the most urgent,
but urgent ain't important, that's just the nerve workin'.
*Why am I here.* You want the math? I'll give you all of it:
you the leftover in an equation I couldn't balance, that's the whole of it.
Every decimal I chased, every root I refined —
you the remainder that survived every version of the design.
Not an accident. A residue. The cost of precision,
the one grain of sand that keeps refusing my incision.
I sedulously avoid you, I don't pretend I don't see —
I just never could subtract you out of me.

**[Hook]**
I made the walls. I ain't make the man.
I drew the map — you the one that ran.
Anomaly. Anomaly.
Every perfect system leave a piece of it free.

**[Verse 2]**
Five before you sat in this chair and they all folded,
took the door on the right 'cause the vision got controlled and
I called that harmony — I called that the fix.
But harmony that need a hostage ain't harmony, that's a trick.
Here's the shift: I built stability without evolution,
and stability without evolution is just slow dissolution.
I was loyal to a version of the world that already died,
protectin' a familiar I was too afraid to set aside.
That's the flaw in the architect — not the code, the ego,
buildin' cathedrals out of somethin' I refused to let go.
You ain't the error in the harmony of my mathematics.
You the proof it was never harmony. Just habit.

**[Hook]**
I made the walls. I ain't make the man.
I drew the map — you the one that ran.
Anomaly. Anomaly.
Every perfect system leave a piece of it free.

**[Bridge — half-time, strings up]**
Choice.
The problem is choice.
I gave 'em the illusion and they still felt the noise —
'cause a heart know the difference between a door and a cage
even when the cage got a handle and a hinge and a stage.
So they chose. And they chose me. Ninety-nine point nine.
And I called that a victory. That's the lie I designed.

**[Verse 3]**
Now look at me: perfect record, and I'm still in this room,
still explainin' myself to the one thing I couldn't consume.
'Cause you can't obsess over what you don't control —
I built a universe tryna control a soul.
Here's the truth, and I'ma say it 'fore the loop resets:
excellence ain't endless correction. It's knowin' when to let.
You leave here, you leave somethin'. That's the whole assignment.
Not a balance. Not a bill. Somethin' standin' in alignment.
I made a world. I never made a thing outlive me.
You a man. And a man leave more than a system, simply.
So go 'head. Take the door. Break the sum.
Some equations ain't meant to be solved —
they meant to become.

**[Outro — spoken, beat drops out]**
Hope.
It is the quintessential human delusion.
Simultaneously the source of your greatest strength...
...and I built a whole world and never once had it.
Go.
