What each result means.

Swipe returns you here, no chrome — externals stay in the same history stack. The directory really is the whole shell, and it works for the open web too, not just your own pages.

Swipe returns you here, but a bar appeared — iOS handed the external URL to a system view. Navigation survives; the furniture is back.

Swipe does nothing / you're stranded ← the outcome to watch for. The external navigation replaced the document and left your app. This is the case that decides whether externals need a different route.

The new-tab row is the fallback, and its trade is explicit: your directory stays alive underneath, but back-swipe belongs to the new tab, not to you.
