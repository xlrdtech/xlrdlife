The lip-sync step costs more than twice the scene generation. That inverts the intuition — the expensive part isn't making the footage, it's making the mouths match. Which is also the one stage you didn't have a tool picked for.

Verify two things before you buy. Higgsfield's tier names differ across sources (Starter/Plus/Ultra vs Pro/Max) and monthly billing runs 21–26% above the annual price shown first on their page. And Grok video access is gated. Both are worth a 60-second check against the vendor's own pricing page.
