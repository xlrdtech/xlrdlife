The scam approach ignore

A Telegram contact named "Mirage" opened with the textbook wrong-number script: "I was trying to message my friend but I lost her number and accidentally dialed the wrong number… Sorry for the mistake… Btw it's me Charlotte, 35 years old and I am from LA, what about you?… Nice to meet you too… May I know your name?"

This is the standard opening sequence of a pig-butchering / romance-investment scam: manufactured accident, immediate self-introduction with age and city, rapid escalation to name exchange. Do not engage. Alongside it, the same app delivered repeated VPN referral spam from media-downloader bots.

What this is evidence of read this part

1. The ratio. Roughly two hundred notifications in eight minutes produced seven items that needed a decision. Everything else was marketing, model-release churn, or a device narrating itself. That is a signal-to-noise ratio of about 3.5% — and every one of the seven was easier to miss because of the other hundred and ninety-three.
2. One account outweighed everything personal. A single social media account generated about 45 notifications in eight minutes — more than every billing notice, security alert, real message and genuine reminder combined. The loudest source in the feed had nothing at stake in it.
3. Repetition inflates the count without adding information. The same items arrived two and three times through different apps — one email client, one mail app and one mobile client each announced the same messages. One real appointment produced four notifications. Deduplicated, the eight minutes hold maybe sixty distinct facts.
