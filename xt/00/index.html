05 / Questions

Frequently asked

01Who owns what you build?+

You do. Everything is delivered as open source with the architecture documented. There is no proprietary runtime you have to keep paying for and no lock-in clause.

02Is xlrd.org really a nonprofit?+

Yes — a registered 501(c)(3). Contributions fund development that is released publicly rather than sold as a private product.

03Do you work with organizations outside the US?+

Yes. The work is remote by default and coordinated through shared repositories and written specifications, so time zone is not a constraint.

04What does a contribution actually pay for?+

Development time on open-source tooling, and the infrastructure that keeps the published work online and free to access.
