Your first steps as a HERO

Six steps, in order. Every tier runs the same sequence — what changes is how much of step 4 we do for you.

  1. 1
    Right after you sign

    Welcome in

    You get your welcome and your bearings — what you just joined, who your people are, and the one thing to do first. No 40-page manual, no scavenger hunt.

    Your W.O.M.E.N.™ intake profile is already on file from enrollment, so nothing gets asked of you twice.

    Your kickoff call gets scheduled here, so nothing stalls between signing and starting.

  2. 2
    Day one

    Access granted

    Two doors open immediately, at every tier:

    Skool Community Membership Where the members are — questions answered, wins posted, nobody building alone.
    L7S University The training library. All seven Heroes are taught here — Credit Hero HoT 7™, CEO Hero, Loan Hero, Cashflow Hero, Crypto Hero, Coverage Hero and Community Hero (what each one does).

    Invite links land with your welcome. If either door does not open, say so in the community and it gets fixed the same day.

  3. 3
    Your first scheduled session

    Kickoff HERO C.A.R.E CALL

    Consulting and Coaching Session Call for Clarity, Confidence and a Clear Path to Conscious Choices for Change — this time pointed at execution rather than discovery.

    You leave it with the same deliverables, updated for where you now stand: your R&R™ Audit (Review & Recommendation — Audit & Analysis Awareness Evaluation), your AIR Recap (Analytical Insights Report), and a refreshed A.B.C.S.™ roadmap with your hero deployment plan.

    • Your priority order — what gets built first, second, third.
    • Your "C" focus confirmed for this phase.
    • Who does what — clear on both sides before any building starts.
  4. 4
    The build

    Systems & Models — Installation & Implementation

    This is the work, and it runs at your tier level:

    • DIY — Do-It-Yourself. You install, using the products and the University to guide each step.
    • DWY — Done-With-You. We install alongside you, with the 7 Core Heroes program running in parallel — the seven deployed through the L7S Hero S.O.S.™ Superhero Operating System.
    • DFY — Done-For-You. We install it for you at the F.A.M.E level, with 1-on-1 and cohort hybrid mentorship on top.

    Which one you are on was settled at enrollment — see the tier breakdown or the DIY → DWY → DFY ladder.

  5. 5
    Go live

    Switch it on

    Your systems get turned on in the real world, with real volume — not left sitting in a sandbox. We watch the first cycle with you and correct what the first cycle reveals.

    Anything that breaks in week one gets fixed in week one.

  6. 6
    Ongoing

    Stay in the cadence

    Onboarding ends. Membership does not.

    • Community — the Skool room stays open to you.
    • L7S University — new material as it is published.
    • Virtual Events, MasterClass & Webinars — GA tickets at member pricing (see your tier).
    • Follow-up HERO C.A.R.E CALLS — book one whenever a new "C" comes up.
No payment is collected on this page. Anything owed was settled at enrollment, over Zelle, CashApp or Crypto, confirmed on your call. Card checkout via Stripe is planned but not live — see how to pay.

What to have ready

None of this is a gate. Having it on hand just means step 3 moves faster.

  • Your entity details — however your business is currently structured.
  • The tools already running — what you use today, even if it is a spreadsheet.
  • Who else touches the work — team, contractors, whoever needs access.
  • Your 90-day "done" — one sentence on what success looks like.

The partner sequence

Four steps, and the first two are non-negotiable.

  1. 1
    Required

    Agreement + NDA

    Partner onboarding does not begin until the partnership agreement is signed and the NDA is executed. Confidentiality comes before access, every time — no exceptions for any of the four partner types.

    JV and Enterprise partners also cover roles and responsibilities, performance pledges and clauses, and rev-share, profit or equity split allocation at this stage.

  2. 2
    Required

    Fit & compatibility review

    We look at compatibility and reputation on both sides before anything is wired up. For Enterprise-level collaborations that includes a Predictive Index assessment.

  3. 3

    Enablement

    You get what you need to actually represent the work — the offer set, the language, the boundaries of what you can say, and your tracking or attribution setup.

    Partner portal and tracking links are being set up — they are handed over directly for now.

  4. 4

    Go live & review cadence

    You start sending or selling, and we review together on a set rhythm — what is converting, what needs adjusting, what to expand.

Partner enquiries go through the same front door. Book a HERO C.A.R.E CALL and say it is about partnership — pick Community or Something else as your "C" category and name the partner type in the form.
