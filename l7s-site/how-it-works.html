The path, start to finish

Five steps. Nothing hidden, nothing you have to guess at.

  1. 1

    Discover

    You find L7S — a referral, an event, a post, a search. Read who we are, look over the four tiers, and see whether the way we work fits the way you work.

    Nothing is asked of you at this stage but a look.

  2. 2
    This is the front door

    Book a HERO C.A.R.E CALL

    Consulting and Coaching Session Call for Clarity, Confidence and a Clear Path to Conscious Choices for Change.

    You pick the "C" category the call is about — Business Credit, Cashflow Confidence, Capital Consulting / Funding, Cryptocurrency Education, Corporation Structuring, Currency Exchange / FX, Community, or another "C". It is not just about credit, and no active credit monitoring is required.

    Booking runs on the W.O.M.E.N.™ intake profile — your business details and objectives, entered once, so the call opens on the real conversation instead of the basics.

  3. 3

    Get your R&R™ Audit and AIR Recap

    R&R™ — Review & Recommendation. In full, the way L7S registers it: the Review & Recommendation Audit & Analysis Awareness Evaluation. In plain English — a straight look at where things stand, an honest read on what you are and are not yet aware of, and the specific moves we would make next.

    AIR Recap — Analytical Insights Report. Your session distilled into insights you can act on and revisit.

    You also leave with a personalised A.B.C.S.™ roadmap and your hero deployment plan — which of the 7 Core Heroes goes to work first.

    All of it comes out of every HERO C.A.R.E CALL. It is yours either way — even if you decide L7S is not your next move.

  4. 4

    Choose your tier — this is enrollment

    With the audit in hand you pick the level that matches where you are: Low Ticket, Mid Ticket, High Ticket, or Enterprise. Each one is a real path forward — see the full tier breakdown and pricing.

    Not sure which fits? That is exactly what the call is for. We map it with you — no pressure, just clarity.

  5. 5
    Getting started

    Onboard and go

    You are welcomed in, given access to the Skool Community and L7S University, and your systems get installed and implemented at your tier level.

    The full sequence lives on its own page — see what onboarding looks like.

For enrollment

How enrollment actually happens

Enrollment is step 4, spelled out. No surprises, no fine-print games.

What you do

  • Book the call. Enrollment starts on a HERO C.A.R.E CALL, not on a checkout page.
  • Pick your tier with your R&R Audit in front of you.
  • Confirm the terms for that tier — membership, deposit, or agreement, depending on the level you choose.
  • Get your access to the Skool Community and L7S University.

What we do

  • Run the call and deliver your R&R Audit and AIR Recap.
  • Recommend a tier — the honest one, not the biggest one.
  • Set up your access and hand you a starting point on day one.
  • Schedule your kickoff so momentum does not stall after you sign.
Tiers and amounts live on one page, on purpose. So nothing here goes stale, every figure — memberships, deposits, and what is included at each level — is kept on the pricing page. Higher tiers add an agreement and signature step; Enterprise is by application and includes a compatibility and reputation review plus a Predictive Index assessment.

The protocol underneath

The A.B.C.S.™ Protocol

The proprietary foundation framework for every engagement. Every tier, every Hero, every call runs on the same four moves — so the work is never improvised.

AAnalytics Data-driven insights, credit-report analysis, business audits and performance scorecards to establish your baseline.
BBlueprints Custom business plans, BXBlueprints™, buildout bundles and strategic roadmaps tailored to your mission.
CChecklists Progress trackers, compliance checklists, P.A.T. D.O.W.N.S.™ protocols and accountability dashboards.
SScaling Systems, sequences, SOPs and L7S automation frameworks to multiply your results and streamline operations.
Your A.B.C.S.™ roadmap comes out of the call. Your R&R™ Audit produces a personalised A.B.C.S.™ roadmap plus the hero deployment plan — which of the seven you are putting to work first, and in what order.
