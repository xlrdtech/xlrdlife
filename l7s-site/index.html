Know who you're working with

CIS / KYC / KYB / KYT — the same structure applied to the individual and the business brand, on both sides of an engagement.

  • CIS — Customer/Company Information Summary: who this is, in one page.
  • KYC — Know Your Customer: identity of the individual behind the work.
  • KYB — Know Your Business: the entity, scope, and what it delivers.
  • KYT — Know Your Transaction: how engagements start, what's in and out of scope.
What L7S is — and is not. L7S is a strategic planning, consulting and coordination firm. L7S is not a credit repair company, tax preparer or advisor, legal or financial advisor, investment advisor or broker-dealer, lender, debt collector or insurance broker. Members are connected with licensed third-party credit specialists, attorneys, accountants and financial advisors who deliver those services in compliance with applicable state and federal law, including CROA and FTC regulations — those services are provided by those licensed professionals, not by L7S, Inc.
