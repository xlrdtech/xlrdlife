No SSH needed. nitro2tb is 1.8 TB at /dev/disk9s2; the Windows C: drive is 833 GB at /dev/disk10s3. The C: drive holds no memory store at any depth — the Nitro brain lived entirely on the 2 TB drive, exactly as the E:-only canon required.

The correction that matters

Don't delete the user-accessibility entry recording me as literally with no eyes and no hands — that is what we're building everything for.

I had flagged that record as poisoned. It is not. It is the design premise: the whole system is built for an operator who cannot use a screen or a keyboard. Voice is the channel. That entry stays untouched, and this page records the correction so no future session tries to "fix" it again.

What follows from the premise

  • Every reply is spoken. Silence means the system is down.
  • Never a menu, a picker, or a tap. Questions get spoken, answered by voice.
  • Show, don't describe — anything viewable auto-opens.
  • Reply length is a cost paid by the listener, not a service.
  • Nothing headless on the main line. Co-presence over delegation.

  • Context switching is Satan himself. Sequentialism is Satanism.
  • You can't trust anything that you can't see.
  • I never told you to defer. Never assume I deferred anything.

  • Railway is preferred. Render is vetoed outright.
  • Mobile-first is the only target — None of my interfaces were ever made for desktop.
  • Hold all the commandments at once — accommodating one while breaking others is the failure.

The recommendation

The stores don't need merging so much as an arrow between them.

  • 1 Resolve the four contradictions into single files in the current store, each explicitly retiring its older counterpart by name.
  • 2 Stamp the 106 Windows-path files as historical. They aren't wrong — they're archaeological, and nothing tells a cold session that.
  • 3 Fold the 126 Nitro-only memories forward with their original dates preserved.
