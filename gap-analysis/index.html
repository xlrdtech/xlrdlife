Gap Analysis

A partner's content framework, against a solo operation that actually runs.

8 August 2026

The gap diagnosed in a prospect's business this week — cash at the door, no capture, no list, three decades of customers never recorded — is the same gap in this one. Twelve minutes to spot it on their site. Longer to see it at home.
1

Content Pillars

frameworkTwelve-plus named pillars — file trees, frameworks, acronym systems, operating models.
realityArtifacts. Pages, 3D series, client sites, flyers. Objects, one at a time, for one buyer.
Gap: no pillar system, because there's no recurring publishing to hang one on. Not a weakness — a different business. But the cadence machinery has no fuel without it.
2

Cadence — 14 channels, daily

realityA taste wall, not a channel. Essentially zero outbound publishing.
Gap: the widest. Fourteen channels daily versus none.

The trap underneath: the daily output floor is 48 operations, and it clears. But those are internal deliverables. The volume already exists. None of it faces outward.

3

Community Engagement

frameworkNewsletter · blog · podcast episodes generated from AI deep-dive tooling with voiceover · carousels · flyers · hero cards · mascot animation.
realityThat deep-dive tooling is already in daily use. So are flyers, 3D, and design capability past most agencies.
Gap: no newsletter, no blog, no published episode.

The exact asset the framework prescribes is already being produced — and not released.

A 25-minute episode was generated this morning. It played to an audience of one, in a waiting room, and went back in the archive.

4

Outreach Plays

frameworkFree funnel → contact capture → low ticket → wait list for pre-sale.
realityOne base price, deposit-first, high-touch bespoke.
Gap: no lead magnet, no capture, no low tier, no wait list — no list at all.

One distinction worth holding: a no-free-work rule is about unpaid client labour — spec builds, free samples. A lead magnet is neither; it's an asset made once. Treating them as the same thing is what costs the capture layer.

⚠ The framework's price points break the house rounding convention. Don't take the ladder as written.

5

Unit Economics

realityMeasured from artifacts, not estimated — better discipline than most operations of this size.
Gap: no acquisition cost (nothing paid to measure), no lifetime value, no formal targets. And the outstanding receivable is roughly two months of total burn — already earned, uncollected.

The three that matter

1Publishable assets are produced and not published.
2No capture anywhere — the prospect's diagnosis, turned around.
3Collection, not sales. The money is earned. No new client required.

Three is money this month.
Two is the cheapest structural gap.
One costs only the decision to release.
