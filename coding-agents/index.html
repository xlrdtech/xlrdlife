The metering question

The ACU story changed — and it got worse, not better.

You remembered Devin as $500/month billed in Agent Compute Units. That intel is stale in a way that matters, so here is exactly what is true today.

What actually changed

Devin no longer bills self-serve customers in ACUs. The pricing page today shows Free $0 / Pro $20 / Max $200 / Teams $80 plus $40 per full dev seat. The self-serve billing doc calls the unit “on-demand credits” with a daily and weekly quota that refreshes on a calendar basis; Max gets a weekly allowance with no daily cap.

I hunted the ACU deliberately. In Devin's full doc index the only surviving ACU pages sit under /federal/ and /admin/billing/enterprise, where the text reads: “Devin Enterprise customers are billed in Agent Compute Units (ACUs) at the rate set in their order form.” ACUs are now a negotiated contract unit for enterprise and government, nothing more.

devin.ai/pricing · docs.devin.ai/admin/billing/enterprise — both read 2026-08-01

⚠ Why this is worse for you than $500 flat was

“Cost per message varies based on the model used, the task size and complexity, and the reasoning required.”

That sentence is from Cognition's own pricing page. They publish no per-credit rate, no quota count, and no overage table. You asked me to quantify the ACU so you could see why you'd hate it. I cannot quantify it — and that is the finding.

A $20 sticker attached to an unpublished, variable, per-message meter is strictly worse than a $500 flat rate, because you cannot compute your worst case before you commit. Your rule is that flat beats metered even at a higher sticker. Devin fails that rule harder now than it did at $500.

The old headline numbers — the $500 launch price, roughly $2.25 per ACU, “1 ACU is about 15 minutes of Devin working” — appear nowhere on Cognition's site today. They are marked UNVERIFIED and I will not repeat them at you as fact.

The finding that touches your $136

Do not point Claude Code at your own endpoints.

The base-URL column splits this market cleanly, and the answer for the one tool you already pay for is the bad one.

⚠ It converts flat into metered

Anthropic's own gateway docs state that while a gateway credential is active, “a developer's claude.ai subscription isn't used… and the subscription's usage limits don't apply. That traffic is billed per token.”

So the move that looks like it saves money is precisely the move that turns your one flat line item into the metering model you refuse.

code.claude.com/docs/en/llm-gateway — read 2026-08-02

And it cannot reach your endpoints anyway

Anthropic does not support routing Claude Code to non-Claude models through any gateway. ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL exists, but the endpoint has to speak the Anthropic Messages API — POST /v1/messages with an anthropic-version header — not the OpenAI shape your :20128 and :20129 serve.

There is a second cost. Remote Control and Claude Code on the web are disabled the moment a gateway credential is set. Your strictly-remote requirement and any BYO-endpoint plan for Claude Code are mutually exclusive — you cannot have both.

Nothing. Buy zero coding tools tonight.

  • “A better agent.” You have five installed and drive one. A sixth doesn't plug into xen-reply-vvs, the 218 launchd jobs, or the goal index — buying one means rebuilding your harness inside someone else's product.
  • “Integrations.” 56 MCP servers. Already done, already free. That is the exact thing paid platforms sell as their differentiator.
  • “Model choice, no lock-in.” OmniRoute already fronts 318 routes with semantic aliases. A paid router is redundant infrastructure.
  • “Automation and scheduling.” 461 plists, watchdogs, a 60-second comms law. No SaaS scheduler beats that for your workflow, and each one is a monthly bill for something already running at $0.
The two real gaps — both close for $0
  • Remote access. This is a gap in your stack, not a reason to buy. Your control surface is a tmux pane on a desk machine — but :20129 already exposes that pane as an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, and Tailscale is already installed.
