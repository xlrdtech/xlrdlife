Measured off the rendered pixels, this load — not read back from the config. THROUGH is how much of the text behind survives inside the orb: with premultiplied alpha the surviving background fraction is exactly 1−α, so 100% is invisible glass and 0% is a solid ball. HALO is the same measurement outside the orb — the wash a version lays over the rest of the page. Lower HALO means it dirties more of the page around it, which costs readability just as much as a solid centre does.
